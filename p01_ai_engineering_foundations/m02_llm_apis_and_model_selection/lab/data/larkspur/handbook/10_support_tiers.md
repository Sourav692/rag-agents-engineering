# Support tiers

Support tickets are answered within 4 hours on business plans and within 24 hours on starter plans.

Enterprise plans answer urgent tickets within 1 hour at any time of day and other tickets within 4 hours. Each Enterprise account has a named support engineer and can reach support by phone.

Open a ticket from the console in one of three categories: Billing for invoices, refunds and credits; Technical for servers, storage and the API; Security for access and suspected incidents. Choosing the right category sends the ticket to the right team first.

Response times are counted from when the ticket is opened, in the hours of the support region that serves the account.

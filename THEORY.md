# Theory

> The index is generated by `scripts/build_curriculum.py`. The pages are hand-written: edit `<part>/<module>/mandatory/theory/README.md` and the lesson folders beside it and in `optional/theory/` (see `CLAUDE.md`, Theory pages).

Each module keeps its theory in its own folder: the module page and the pages of lessons with a mandatory notebook in `mandatory/theory/`, the pages of all-optional lessons in `optional/theory/`. A module page has the overview, interview questions and takeaways; each lesson page explains that lesson's concepts with diagrams. Each module's `index.html` joins its pages into one page to read in a browser. Some lessons have interactive labs (single HTML files, open them in a browser).

## The page

Page 1 holds Figure 1 (annual wildfires and acres burned, 1993 to 2022, a line and area chart), Table 1 (annual wildfires and acres burned, 2018 to 2022) and Figure 2 (the five years with the largest acreage burned since 1960, a bar chart with labelled values).

## questions.jsonl

Fields: `id`, `question`, `answer` (as printed on the page), `check` (the values the grader looks for as numbers) and `source` (the figure or table). Questions `q1` to `q3` read Figure 2 and `q4` and `q5` read Table 1.

A misrouted ticket is filtered to a knowledge base that does not hold its answer, so retrieval there cannot find the evidence. A fallback searches all three knowledge bases, which costs three searches instead of one. When every score ties at 0.000, the router takes the first in its list, billing, as a classifier forced to pick one label would.

The knowledge bases are the twelve handbook chunks from the M11 lab, five in billing, four in technical and three in general. TF-IDF uses scikit-learn’s defaults with English stop words.

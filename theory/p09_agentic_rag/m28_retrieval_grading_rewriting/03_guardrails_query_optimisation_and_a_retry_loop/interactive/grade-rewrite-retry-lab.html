What is real here

Retrieval is real: TF-IDF with scikit-learn’s defaults over the twelve M11 handbook chunks, top 2 as in notebook 03a. The routing and the call counts follow 03a: one call for the topic check, one grader call per chunk, one per rewrite, one to generate; the handlers are fixed text. Three parts are stand-ins. The grader passes a chunk only if the golden set lists it as the answer, the topic check reads the golden label, and the rewrites are hand-written in the style the 03a optimiser prompt asks for. A real LLM grader and rewriter make mistakes these do not.

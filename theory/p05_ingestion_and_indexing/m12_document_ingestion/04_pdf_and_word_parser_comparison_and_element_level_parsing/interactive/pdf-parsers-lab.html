5. Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded; monthly plans cannot. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

  • Within 30 days: full refund if outbound traffic is under 100 GB.
  • After day 30: unused whole months, minus a 15% early-termination fee.
  • SLA and promotional credits are never paid out as cash.

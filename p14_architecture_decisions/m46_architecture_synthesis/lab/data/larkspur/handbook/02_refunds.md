# Refunds

Monthly plans are not refundable. If you cancel a monthly plan halfway through a cycle, the servers stay online until the cycle ends and then shut down.

An annual plan can get a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the account used less than 100 GB of outbound traffic.

After day 30, annual refunds are prorated: you get back the unused whole months, minus a 15% early-termination fee.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement.

To request a refund, open a ticket in the Billing category and include the invoice number. Requests sent by email are not tracked and take longer.

# Onboarding

To start, create an account with an email address and confirm it from the link Larkspur sends. The person who creates the account becomes its owner.

New accounts get a 14-day Starter trial with 50 USD of trial credit. Trial credit is a promotional credit, and it covers servers in any region.

Before the first server starts, add a payment method on the Billing page. Larkspur does not charge the card until the trial credit runs out or the trial ends, whichever comes first.

Create a first server from the console: choose a region, a size and an operating system image. A new server is usually ready within two minutes.

Invite teammates from Settings, then Team. Team accounts are available on Business and Enterprise plans, and each teammate signs in with their own login.

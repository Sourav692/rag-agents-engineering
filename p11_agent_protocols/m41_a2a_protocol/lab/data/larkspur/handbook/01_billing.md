# Billing and payments

Monthly plans are billed at the start of each billing cycle. Annual plans are billed once a year on the purchase date.

Every invoice is emailed to the account owner on the billing date and stays available on the Billing page. Larkspur accepts credit cards on every plan. Enterprise accounts can also pay by bank transfer, with invoices due 30 days after issue.

Each server size includes an outbound traffic allowance. Traffic above the allowance is billed at 0.01 USD per GB at the end of the cycle in which it was used. Inbound traffic is free.

If a payment fails, Larkspur retries the card three times over 7 days and emails the account owner after each attempt. When the last retry fails, servers are suspended but not deleted, and they start again as soon as the balance is paid.

You can cancel any plan from the Billing page. Cancelling stops the next charge, and servers keep running until the paid period ends.

Promotional credits expire 12 months after they are issued and cannot be paid out as cash.

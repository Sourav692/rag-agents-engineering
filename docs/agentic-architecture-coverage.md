# Agentic Architecture Coverage

Comparison of [FareedKhan-dev/all-agentic-architectures](https://github.com/FareedKhan-dev/all-agentic-architectures) (35 architectures) with this course (45 modules, nine source repos). The 35 architectures are taught by notebooks from repos 06, 08 and 09; paths and module numbers below are the course's (`course/<part>/<module>/<folder>/<file>`).

## Verdict

**All 35 architectures are covered.** 33 have a dedicated notebook, 1 is merged into another notebook (PEV), and 1 is taught as an adaptation (RLHF self-improvement).

**20 are on the mandatory track; 15 are taught only in optional notebooks** (#10 Mental Loop (simulator), #11 Meta-Controller, #12 Graph (world-model) Memory, #13 Ensemble, #15 RLHF Self-Improvement, #16 Cellular Automata, #17 Reflexive Metacognitive, #19 Self-Discover, #22 LATS, #28 Debate, #29 Voyager (skill library), #32 Constitutional AI, #33 SWE-Agent, #34 Computer Use / Browser Agent, #35 Agent Workflow Memory). Optional notebooks are marked *(optional)* in the table.

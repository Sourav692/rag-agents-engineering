Staff-Level AI System Design Roadmap

18 domains · 56 modules · Fundamental / Intermediate / Advanced / Optional

Comprehensive preparation for Staff FDE, Applied AI Engineer, AI Platform Engineer and AI Lab infrastructure roles.

How to study

Pass 1: Finish every Fundamental topic. Pass 2: Finish every Intermediate topic. Pass 3: Select Advanced depth for your target role. Pass 4: Explore Optional specializations if needed.

Role focus: FDE → Traffic, Security, Reliability, AI Platform, Evaluation; AI Labs → Inference, GPU, Training, Distributed Systems.

Capstone exercises

  1. Multi-tenant AI gateway with fair scheduling and backpressure
  2. Chat-style streaming backend with persistence and low tail latency
  3. Enterprise search and ACL-aware indexing platform
  4. Real-time voice application and end-to-end latency budgets
  5. GPU model-serving and quota-based scheduling platform
  6. Multi-tenant fine-tuning and model registry platform
  7. AI evaluation and release-gating platform
  8. Multi-region AI platform with tenant isolation and cost controls
  9. AI SRE incident analysis and recovery architecture
  10. Scale an AI MVP to 100 enterprise customers

Completion criteria

Fundamental: Explain what/why/when, draw a diagram, and answer three conceptual questions. Intermediate: Design a realistic service, compare alternatives, explain failure handling and metrics. Advanced: defend a design under competing and changing constraints. Optional: prove specialized implementation depth.

# Account deletion

Only the account owner can delete an account. The owner starts the deletion from Settings, then Account, and confirms it with a code sent to the owner's email address.

Deleting an account cancels every plan on it, so all of its data follows the retention rule for cancelled plans. Export what you need first, because the owner can no longer sign in once the deletion is confirmed.

Contact details and team members are removed within 30 days of the deletion. Invoices are kept for 7 years, as tax law requires, and stay available on request through the Billing category.

An account with an unpaid balance cannot be deleted until the balance is paid.

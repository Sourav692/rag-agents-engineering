# SLA and service credits

Larkspur guarantees 99.95% monthly availability. If an outage pushes availability below that, you receive service credits on the next invoice.

Availability is measured per region by the status page probes, and maintenance announced 7 days ahead does not count against it.

When availability in a region falls below 99.95%, affected accounts are owed credits worth 10% of that month's server charges in that region, or 25% when it falls below 99.0%.

The credits are not added automatically. You claim them by opening a ticket in the Billing category within 30 days of the incident.

Service credits are applied to the next invoice and cannot be paid out as cash. Credits for one month never exceed that month's server charges.

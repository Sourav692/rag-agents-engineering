# Community forum: How fast are Larkspur refunds?

Posted by a Larkspur customer in the community forum. This is not an official Larkspur page.

I asked for a refund on my annual plan last spring and it was back on my card in 2 to 3 business days. Support told me that is the normal refund time for every plan, so there is no need to wait longer than that before chasing them.
